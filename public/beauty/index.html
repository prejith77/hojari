Fair questions

How much do booking apps charge a salon?

In the UK, on their own pricing pages read on 17 November 2026: Fresha takes 20% of a new marketplace client’s first booking (minimum £4), Booksy 30% plus VAT (minimum £5) if you switch on its Boost feature, and Treatwell 35% plus VAT, on top of monthly plans and card fees. See the guides for your country

Can I keep Treatwell or Fresha as well?

Yes. Many salons keep a marketplace to be found by new clients and send their regulars to book on their own website, where there’s no commission. I can set it up that way.

Do my clients need an app?

No. It works in the browser on any phone, tablet or computer.

How do deposits get paid?

By card, through a payment provider such as Stripe, straight into your own account. Your provider’s card fee applies; in the UK, Stripe charges 1.5% + 20p on standard cards.

Do I own it?

Yes. Your domain, your words, your photos and your customer data are in your name from day one, and the code is yours once the build is paid.
