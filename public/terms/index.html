The Hojari terms

These are the terms behind every build, in plain English. Your written agreement says the same thing, with your price and dates filled in, and I sign it.

1. You try it before you pay

Once we’ve talked through what you need, and I have your words, photos and prices, I build a working version on a private link and set it up with your business. You use it for seven days, starting the day I send you the link. If you want it, you pay and it moves to your own domain. If it isn’t for you, you owe nothing: no deposit and no fee.

So that I can do each one properly, the free trial has limits. It’s one trial per business. I run a few at a time, so there may be a short wait. On day seven you tell me yes or no; if it’s a no, or I don’t hear from you, the private link is switched off. Until you’ve paid, the working version and its design stay mine and aren’t to be copied or used elsewhere. For bigger builds, the trial shows the first job working, not the whole thing.

2. The price is fixed

You get a fixed price in writing before I start. It only changes if you ask for something new, and you’ll know that cost before I do it. The build is paid once. You can spread it over three monthly payments at no extra cost.

3. Results, or your build fee back

Before I start, we agree in writing up to three numbers to measure, picked to suit your business: what you spend on software and commission, the hours spent on admin, how long customers wait for a reply, or the bookings, orders and enquiries that come in online. I record each one before the build, from figures you give me. Thirty days after launch I measure them again and show you both sets.

If none of the agreed numbers has improved, tell me within 14 days of seeing them and I refund the build fee you’ve paid, within 14 days. The promise covers the build fee, not care. It applies when the site has been live and in use for the full 30 days, and you’ve given me the figures I need to measure. If you take the refund, the build comes down from your domain and care stops; your domain, words, photos and customer data stay yours. This is on top of your legal rights, not instead of them.

4. When it goes live

For websites and one-week fixes, your working version is ready within 7 days of your words and photos reaching me. For bigger tools, within 14 days. Once you have tried it and paid, it moves to your own domain. If I miss the date, your first month of care is free.

5. Care, and leaving

Care is a fixed monthly fee covering hosting, security, backups, updates, small changes, one improvement a month and a one-page monthly report. The first three months are a minimum. After that it runs month to month, and you can leave any time. If you leave, I hand everything over and help you move it.

6. What you own

Your domain, words, photos and customer data are yours from day one, in your name. The code I write for you becomes yours once the build is paid.

7. Replies and fixes

You deal with me, Prejith, throughout. You’ll hear back within one working day. Anything that stops your customers booking, buying or getting in touch is fixed first.

8. Founding clients and referrals

Founding clients, the first five clients up to 31 March 2027, get 30% off the build in return for a short case study once it’s live, and permission to quote what they say about it. Their care price stays the same for their first 12 months, then moves to the normal rate. Reviews are never part of the deal: if you choose to leave one, it’s your own, unpaid opinion. If you recommend a business and they go ahead, they get 10% off their build and you get a month of care free.

9. What I need from you

Your words, photos, logo and any logins I need, and quick answers when I ask a question. If something on your side holds things up, the live date moves by the same amount.
