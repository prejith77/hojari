Fair questions

Do my customers need an app?

No. It works in the browser on any phone, tablet or computer. If you’d like it on their home screen, it can save there like an app.

How do deposits get paid?

By card, through a payment provider such as Stripe, straight into your own account. The provider’s standard card fees apply.

I already pay for a booking app. Can I move?

Yes. I’ll show you the saving, in pounds, before you decide, and move your services and bookings across for you.

Do I own it?

Yes. Your domain, your words, your photos and your customer data are in your name from day one, and the code is yours once the build is paid.
