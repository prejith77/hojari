Fair questions

How much do marketplaces and delivery apps take?

In the UK, on their own pages read on 17 November 2026: Just Eat takes 14% plus VAT with your own drivers and 30% plus VAT when it delivers. Etsy takes 6.5% of the sale and postage, plus 4% + 20p for payment and a 0.48% UK fee. See the guides for your country

Should I leave Etsy or Amazon?

Not necessarily. They’re good at finding new customers. Your own site is where regulars order without the fee. Most small sellers do well with both.

Can customers pay online?

Yes, by card through a provider such as Stripe or Square, straight into your own account.

Do I own it?

Yes. Your domain, your words, your photos and your customer data are in your name from day one, and the code is yours once the build is paid.
