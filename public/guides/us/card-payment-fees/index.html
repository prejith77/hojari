On their own US pages, read on 17 November 2026, taking a card in person costs about 2.3% to 2.7% plus a few cents: PayPal 2.29% + 9¢, Stripe 2.7% + 5¢, and Square and SumUp 2.6% plus 10¢ to 15¢. Online, Stripe and Shopify Basic charge 2.9% + 30¢, Square’s free plan 3.3% + 30¢ and SumUp 3.5% + 15¢. Readers start at $29.

An example: $10,000 a month

About $46 a month between cheapest and dearest. Monthly plans, hardware and contracts often matter more. Clover is widely used too, but its pricing pages wouldn’t load for my check, so it isn’t here.

What matters more than the rate

The honest bit. On $10,000 a month the rate gap is about $10 a week. What saves more is taking the deposit when customers book or order online, so fewer people don’t show and fewer invoices go unpaid.
