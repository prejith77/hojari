One order, two ways

A $40 delivery order on DoorDash Premier or Uber Eats Premium: 30% is $12. The same order on your own website, paid by card through Stripe at 2.9% + 30¢: $1.46. You’d need to deliver it, use a delivery service, or offer pickup.

The apps bring customers who are already looking. Most restaurants do best with both: the apps to be found, and their own ordering for regulars and catering.
