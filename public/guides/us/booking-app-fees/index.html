On their own US pages, read on 17 November 2026: Booksy takes 30% of a client’s first visit if you switch on its Boost feature (up to $100), StyleSeat 30% of a new client’s first appointment if you switch on New Client Connection (up to $50), and Fresha 20% (minimum $6). Vagaro takes nothing from its marketplace. Entry plans for one person run from $19.95 (Fresha) to $35 (StyleSeat); Square has a free plan and Mindbody starts at $79. Card fees run from about 2.3% to 2.6% plus 10¢ to 30¢ in person.

When owning it makes sense

The honest bit. If you want a marketplace, Vagaro’s no-commission model is hard to argue with, and I’d tell you so. Your own booking makes sense once most clients are regulars: they book and pay a deposit on your own website, under your name, and no app sits between you.
