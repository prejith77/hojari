What lead sites charge a contractor

By Prejith Ponnachan · Updated 17 November 2026

Most US lead sites don’t publish a price per lead. Angi charges a fee per lead that varies with the job. Thumbtack lets pros set their own lead price. Porch gives examples from $10 for a handyman lead to $60 for a kitchen remodel. Yelp Ads start from $150 a month. Nextdoor business pages are free. All read on their own pages or help centres on 17 November 2026.

Work out the real cost

Add up three months of lead fees and divide by the jobs you actually won. If you spent $900 and won six jobs, each job cost you $150 to get. Compare that with what each job is worth to you.

The honest bit. Lead sites help a new business with no reviews yet. Your own website catches the people who search your name after a neighbour mentions you, and the repeat customers and referrals that should never cost you a fee.
