For booking, Calendly is free for one calendar and one type of meeting, and its Standard plan is $12 a month per seat, or $10 billed yearly, in US dollars. Course platforms mostly price in US dollars too: Teachable from $39 a month with a 7.5% fee on its Starter plan, Podia from $49 with a 5% fee, and Kajabi from $179, with no revenue share if you use its own payments. Tutorful takes no commission from tutors; students pay a service fee on top. All read on their own pages on 17 November 2026.

When owning it makes sense

The honest bit. If you only need people to book a call, Calendly’s free plan may be all you need. A site of your own is worth it when your website is where clients decide to trust you, and you want booking, payment and your course or programme in one place, under your name, without a percentage on every sale.
