Five questions before you sign

  • What happens to the price as you grow?

    Some charge per location, some per active customer, some by quote. Work out the price at double your members, not just today.

  • What do card payments really cost?

    Ask for the UK rate in writing, and whether the app adds its own fee on top of the payment provider’s.

  • Is there a contract?

    Monthly with no contract, or a year up front. Price on request usually means a contract.

  • Who pays the marketplace fee?

    If the app lists you in its own marketplace, find out what it takes from new members it sends.

  • Can you take your members with you?

    Check you can export your member list and history, in full, whenever you like.

When owning it makes sense

The honest bit. For a small studio, an app like Gymcatch or TeamUp is hard to beat on price, and I’d tell you so. Your own system makes sense when the app doesn’t fit how you run classes, when you’re paying for several apps that don’t talk to each other, or when you want sign-ups, memberships and payments on your own website, under your name.
