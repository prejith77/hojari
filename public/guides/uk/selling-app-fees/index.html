One sale, two ways

A £30 item with £4 postage, sold on Etsy: the transaction fee is 6.5% of £34, so £2.21. Payment processing is 4% of £34 plus 20p, so £1.56. The regulatory fee is 16p. That’s £3.93, before VAT and the listing fee.

The same sale on your own website, paid with a standard UK card through Stripe: 1.5% of £34 plus 20p, so 71p.

The difference is real, but so is what a marketplace gives you: buyers who were already looking. On your own site, you bring the customers. Most small sellers do best with both, using the marketplace to be found and their own site for regulars.
