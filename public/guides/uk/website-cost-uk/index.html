What a small business website costs in the UK

By Prejith Ponnachan · Updated 17 November 2026

You can build it yourself on a website builder, for about £12 to £25 a month plus a domain, or have it made for a one-off fee plus a monthly charge for hosting and care. With Hojari, a website with one tool, such as booking or quotes, costs from £750, then £49 a month for care.

Which is right for you?

  • Build it yourself

    If you have the time, your needs are simple, and a template that looks like many others is fine.

  • Have it made

    If you need booking, quotes or orders working properly, want it to look like your business and nobody else’s, and would rather spend the hours on customers.

  • Try before you decide

    With Hojari you use a working version in your business for 7 days before you pay anything. If it isn’t right, you owe nothing.
