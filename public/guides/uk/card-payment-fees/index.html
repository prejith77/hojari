On their own UK pages, read on 17 November 2026, taking a card in person costs 1.59% to 1.75% with most providers: Teya 1.59%, SumUp 1.69%, Square and Zettle 1.75%, and Stripe 1.4% + 10p. Card readers start at £19 plus VAT. Online, Square charges 1.4% + 25p, Stripe 1.5% + 20p and SumUp 2.5%. The bigger differences are in monthly plans, minimum-turnover fees and contracts.

Teya’s figure assumes its own business account; paid into another bank it is £50.70 a month. The gap between cheapest and dearest is under £5 a month. Premium and international cards, contracts and minimum-turnover fees change the picture far more than the headline rate.

What matters more than the rate

The honest bit. For most small businesses, the card rate differences are a few pounds a month. Watch the fixed costs instead: monthly plans, minimum-turnover fees, machine rental and contracts you can’t leave. And if customers can pay a deposit online when they book or order, you chase fewer no-shows and fewer unpaid invoices, which is worth more than any rate.
