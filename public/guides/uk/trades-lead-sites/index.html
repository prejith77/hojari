What lead sites charge a tradesperson

By Prejith Ponnachan · Updated 17 November 2026

Lead sites charge UK trades in two ways: a membership, or a fee for each lead. Checkatrade’s paid plans start at £30 a month on a 12-month membership. TrustATrader charges a yearly fee it doesn’t publish. Bark sells credits you spend on leads, and MyBuilder charges a fee each time a customer shortlists you. Most don’t publish what a single lead costs, so you only see it once you’ve joined.

Work out the real cost

The number that matters isn’t the membership or the price of a lead. It’s what you pay for each job you actually win. Add up three months of fees and divide by the jobs that came from the site. If you won four jobs from £360 of fees, each job cost you £90 to get.

Then compare that with what the job is worth to you, and with what the same customers would have cost if they’d found you directly.

The honest bit. Lead sites can work, especially for a new business with no reviews yet. A website of your own won’t replace them overnight. What it does is catch the people who search your name after hearing about you, and the repeat customers and referrals that should never cost you a fee.
