On their own UK pricing pages, read on 17 November 2026, job, quote and invoice apps for trades run from free to about £45 a month on their entry plans. Some charge per user and some per business. ServiceM8 is free for one person doing up to 30 jobs a month, Tradify starts at £34 per user, and Fergus from £39 a month. Most also take about 1.6% to 2% plus 20p when a customer pays with a standard UK card; business and overseas cards cost more.

When owning it makes sense

The honest bit. If one of these apps already runs your jobs well, keep it. They’re good, and switching costs you time. Owning your own makes sense when you want customers to price and accept jobs on your website without ringing you, or when you’re paying per user for features nobody uses.
