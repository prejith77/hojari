A UPI payment made straight to your bank or app QR code carries no merchant fee in India today. A standard 0.4% fee on merchant payments above ₹2,000 was announced in September 2026 and then deferred. No start date had been reported by 14 November 2026, and one could be announced at any time, so check NPCI before you rely on this. Payment gateways for your website charge about 2% plus 18% GST, on UPI as well as cards: Razorpay 2%, PayU 2%, Paytm 1.99% and Cashfree 1.95%.

What matters more than the rate

The honest bit. In your shop, a UPI QR code from your bank or UPI app costs nothing today, so use it. A gateway matters when customers pay on your website, for a booking, an order or a deposit. That’s where a 2% fee buys you something: money taken up front, so fewer people don’t turn up and fewer bills go unpaid.
