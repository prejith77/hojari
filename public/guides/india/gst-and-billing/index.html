You must register for GST once your turnover passes ₹20 lakh if you supply services, or ₹40 lakh if you only supply goods, with lower limits in some states. E-invoicing applies above ₹5 crore turnover. For billing, Zoho Books is free for one user while revenue stays under ₹25 lakh a year, ClearOne is free for now, and myBillBook was showing ₹3,490 a year, on their own pages read on 17 November 2026.

The GST rules

RuleWhat it says
Registration, servicesAbove ₹20 lakh aggregate turnover. ₹10 lakh in Manipur, Mizoram, Nagaland and Tripura
Registration, goods onlyAbove ₹40 lakh. ₹20 lakh in some states, including Telangana, Uttarakhand and Puducherry
E-invoicingAbove ₹5 crore aggregate turnover

Some businesses must register at any turnover, for example if they sell to other states through an online marketplace. Small sellers who sell only within their own state through a marketplace can enrol on the GST portal instead. This is a summary, not tax advice: check the GST portal or a chartered accountant.
