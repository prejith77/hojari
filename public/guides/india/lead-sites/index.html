What lead sites and listings charge

By Prejith Ponnachan · Updated 17 November 2026

IndiaMART publishes its seller plans: ₹40,000 a year for the basic paid membership, up to ₹85,000 a year for Maximiser Pro, on its own help pages updated October 2026. Sulekha charges per lead, at a price that depends on the request and the area. Justdial doesn’t publish its packages. Urban Company’s last published commission range for service partners was 8.5% to 25%, in 2021.

Work out the real cost

Divide what you pay in a year by the customers who actually bought from you through the site. On IndiaMART’s ₹40,000 plan, 20 real customers a year is ₹2,000 each; 5 is ₹8,000 each.

The honest bit. Listings help people find you when they don’t know your name. Your own website is where they check you out afterwards, and where regulars and referrals should reach you without anyone taking a cut.
