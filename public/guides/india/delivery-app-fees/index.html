What Swiggy, Zomato and marketplaces take

By Prejith Ponnachan · Updated 17 November 2026

Neither Swiggy nor Zomato publishes its commission. News reports put it between 8% and 30% of the order, with 18% GST on the commission and about 2% for payment on top. For shops, Amazon.in now charges no referral fee on products under ₹1,000 in more than 1,800 categories, and Flipkart charges no commission on products under ₹1,000, according to their own pages read on 17 November 2026.

One order, two ways

A ₹500 food order at a 25% commission, the middle of the reported range: ₹125, plus ₹22.50 GST on the commission, plus about ₹10 for payment. That’s about ₹157.50 gone from a ₹500 order.

The same order on your own website, paid through a gateway such as Cashfree at 1.95% plus GST: about ₹11.50. Or paid by UPI to your own QR code, which is free today. You’d deliver it yourself, or offer pick-up.

The apps bring customers who are already looking. Most restaurants do best with both: the apps to be found, and their own ordering on WhatsApp or their website for regulars.
