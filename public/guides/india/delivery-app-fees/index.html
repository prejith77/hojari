I found no public rate card for Swiggy or Zomato. A Bengaluru restaurant association put their commission at 8% to 28% of the order in July 2026, and an earlier report put it at 16% to 30%. For shops, Amazon.in charges no referral fee on most products under ₹1,000, and Flipkart announced no commission for eligible sellers on products under ₹1,000.

One order, two ways

A ₹500 food order at a 25% commission, the typical figure in The Week’s report: ₹125, plus ₹22.50 GST on the commission, plus about ₹10 for payment. That’s about ₹157.50 gone from a ₹500 order.

The same order on your own website, paid through a gateway such as Cashfree at 1.95% plus GST: about ₹11.50. Or paid by UPI to your own QR code, which is free today. You’d deliver it yourself, or offer pick-up.

The apps bring customers who are already looking. Most restaurants do best with both: the apps to be found, and their own ordering on WhatsApp or their website for regulars.
