On their own Canadian pages, read on 17 November 2026: Square charges 2.5% on in-person credit cards and 0.75% + 7¢ on debit. Stripe charges 2.7% + 5¢ in person, 15¢ per Interac debit and 2.9% + 30¢ online. Helcim charges interchange plus 0.40% + 8¢ in person at lower volumes, and 9¢ per inserted Interac payment. None has a monthly fee on its basic plan.

What matters more than the rate

The honest bit. Interac debit is far cheaper than credit with all three providers here, so a business with lots of debit customers pays much less than the headline rate suggests. Watch contracts, monthly plans and hardware leases more than a tenth of a percent. And deposits taken online when people book or order mean fewer no-shows and fewer unpaid invoices.
