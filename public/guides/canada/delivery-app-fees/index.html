On their own Canadian pages, read on 17 November 2026: Uber Eats takes 20%, 25% or 30% on delivery orders depending on the plan, and DoorDash 20%, 25% or 29%. Both charge less on pickup orders. I found no published rates for SkipTheDishes. Etsy takes 6.5% of the order plus 3% + 25¢ for payment, and Amazon.ca takes 15% in most categories.

One order, two ways

A $40 delivery order through Uber Eats on its Plus plan: 25% is $10. The same order on your own website, paid by card through Stripe in Canada at 2.9% + 30¢: $1.46. You’d still need to deliver it, or offer pickup.

The apps bring customers who are already hungry and looking. Most restaurants do best with both: the apps to be found, and their own ordering for regulars.
