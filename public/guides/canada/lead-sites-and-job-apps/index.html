Work out the real cost

Add up three months of lead fees and divide by the jobs you actually won from them. That number, not the price of a single lead, tells you whether a site is worth it.

The honest bit. Lead sites help a new business that has no reviews yet. Your own website catches the people who search your name after hearing about you, and the repeat customers and referrals that should never cost you a fee.
