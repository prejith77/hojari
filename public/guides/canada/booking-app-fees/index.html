On their own Canadian pages, read on 17 November 2026: Square Appointments is free for one location, or $35 or $85 a month per location, with 2.8% + 30¢ on online payments and 0.75% + 7¢ on debit. Jane, built for clinics, charges 2.5% on in-person credit cards, 10¢ per Interac debit and 2.75% online. Several popular apps don’t publish Canadian prices at all.

When owning it makes sense

The honest bit. Square’s free plan is a strong start for a single-chair salon, and I’d tell you to use it. Your own booking makes sense when you want clients to book on your own website, under your name, with deposits, reminders and every client record in one place you own.
