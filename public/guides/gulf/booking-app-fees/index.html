What booking apps charge a UAE salon

By Prejith Ponnachan · Updated 17 November 2026

Most booking apps don’t publish prices in dirhams. On its own pricing page, read on 17 November 2026, Fresha charges $19.95 a month for one person or $14.95 per team member, in US dollars, plus a 20% one-time commission, minimum $6, on new clients from its marketplace. Salonist starts at $59 a month. Zenoti and Pabau quote on request, and Booksy doesn’t list the UAE.

When owning it makes sense

The honest bit. Fresha’s marketplace can bring you new clients, and for a new salon that may be worth 20% of a first visit. Your own booking makes sense once most of your clients are regulars: they book on your website, pay a deposit and get a reminder, and nobody takes a share of the visit.
