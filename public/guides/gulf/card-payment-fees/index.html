On their own UAE pages, read on 17 November 2026: Ziina charges 2.6% + AED 1 per payment, VAT included; Tap 2.75% + AED 1 on local cards, plus VAT; Stripe 2.9% + AED 1, with no monthly fee. Telr adds a monthly fee of up to AED 249, depending on your sales. Checkout.com quotes each business separately, and Network International’s site wouldn’t open for my check.

An example: AED 8,000 a month

At this size they all land within about AED 40 of each other. Above AED 10,000 a month Telr moves to a per-payment fee, so redo the sums for your own numbers.

What matters more than the rate

The honest bit. Many small Gulf businesses get paid by a payment link sent on WhatsApp, and Ziina, Stripe and Telr all offer one. What saves you more is taking the deposit when the customer books or orders, so fewer people don’t turn up and fewer invoices sit unpaid.
