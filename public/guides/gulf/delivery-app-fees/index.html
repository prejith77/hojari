None of the big delivery apps publishes a standard commission rate for the UAE or Oman. Talabat’s own terms, read on 17 November 2026, say each restaurant’s registration fee, monthly fee and commission are set in its private agreement. I found no published rate for Deliveroo, Careem or Keeta either. For shops, Amazon.ae publishes its fees: most categories pay 5% to 15% of the sale, with no monthly fee for now.

So the most useful thing you can do before you sign is ask each app for its full rate card in writing: commission, monthly fee, registration fee, payment fee and any marketing charges, and compare them on the same order value.

One sale, two ways

A AED 200 kitchen item sold on Amazon.ae: 15% is AED 30, before VAT. The same sale on your own website, paid by card through Stripe in the UAE at 2.9% + AED 1: AED 6.80, plus 5% VAT on that fee unless you’ve given Stripe a UAE VAT number.

Marketplaces and delivery apps bring customers who are already looking, and in the Gulf a lot of ordering happens through them. Most businesses do best with both: the apps to be found, and their own site for regulars, catering and bigger orders.
