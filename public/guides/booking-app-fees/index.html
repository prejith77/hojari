What booking apps charge a UK salon

By Prejith Ponnachan · Updated 17 November 2026

Booking apps charge in three ways: a monthly plan, a commission on new clients their marketplace sends you, and a fee on card payments. On their own UK pricing pages, read on 17 November 2026, the commission on a new client’s first booking runs from 20% (Fresha, minimum £4) to 35% plus VAT (Treatwell), and online card fees from 1.29% + 20p plus VAT (Booksy) to 2.5% plus VAT (Treatwell).

Treatwell charges 0% on repeat clients and on bookings that come through your own website or social media. Fresha’s 20% is charged once per brand-new client found on its marketplace.

An example salon

Take a salon with two stylists that gets five new clients a month through a marketplace, each spending £50 on their first visit. Over a year, before VAT and card fees:

This is an illustration, not a quote. Your numbers depend on how many new clients the marketplace really sends you, and those clients are worth something: a marketplace can fill gaps that your own website won’t.

What owning your booking costs instead

Booking on your own website has no commission. You pay for the site, then a card fee on each payment: Stripe’s standard rate for UK cards is 1.5% + 20p.

With Hojari, a website with online booking and deposits costs from £750, then £49 a month for care, so £1,338 in the first year and £588 a year after that. It doesn’t bring you new clients the way a marketplace can. Many salons keep a marketplace to be found and send their regulars to book on their own site, where nobody takes a cut.
