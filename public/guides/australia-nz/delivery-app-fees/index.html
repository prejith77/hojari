On their own pages, read on 17 November 2026: Uber Eats takes 30% when it delivers in Australia and New Zealand, 16% if you deliver and 8% on pick-up, plus a $700 activation fee. DoorDash takes 30% on delivery, 15% on pick-up in Australia and 14% in New Zealand, with no start-up fee. Amazon Australia takes 6% to 15% of most sales. One order, two ways

A $40 delivery order through Uber Eats or DoorDash, delivered by them: 30% is $12. The same order on your own website, paid by card through Stripe in Australia at 1.65% + 30c: 96c. You’d need to deliver it yourself, or offer pick-up.

The apps bring hungry customers who are already looking. Most restaurants do best with both: the apps to be found, and their own ordering for regulars.
