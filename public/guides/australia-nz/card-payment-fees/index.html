Rates shown are for domestic cards; international cards cost more. With card surcharges gone, this is now a cost to you, not your customers. $50 a month between the cheapest and dearest is $600 a year.

What matters more than the rate

The honest bit. Rental, lock-in contracts and minimum turnovers often cost more than the rate. Ask for the full cost in writing, including GST. And taking a deposit online when people book or order means fewer no-shows, which is worth more than any rate.
