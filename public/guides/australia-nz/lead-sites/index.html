Work out the real cost

Add up three months of lead fees or service fees and divide by the jobs you actually won. That number tells you whether a site is worth it.

The honest bit. Lead sites help a new business with no reviews yet. Your own website catches the people who search your name after hearing about you, and the repeat customers and referrals that should never cost you a fee.
