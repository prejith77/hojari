The surcharge ban

Zeller says surcharges on eftpos, Mastercard, Visa and Amex cards are banned in Australia from 1 October 2026, and it switched off its own surcharge setting that day. If your booking or payment app passed card fees on to clients, that cost is now yours. Ask your provider what changed. In New Zealand, Shift4 (formerly Smartpay) says surcharging remains legal.

When owning it makes sense

The honest bit. Square’s free plan is hard to beat for one chair, and I’d tell you to start there. Your own booking makes sense when you want clients booking and paying deposits on your own website, under your name, with every client record in one place you own.
