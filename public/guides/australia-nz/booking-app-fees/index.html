The surcharge rules

Since 1 October 2026, card network rules stop Australian businesses adding a surcharge for paying by eftpos, Mastercard, Visa or American Express. The Reserve Bank removed its own rule that had prevented those bans, and it’s enforced through your merchant agreement, not by a law against surcharging. Weekend, public holiday, booking and service fees aren’t affected.

If your booking or payment app passed card fees on to clients, that cost is now yours, so ask your provider what changed. In New Zealand, Shift4 (formerly Smartpay) says surcharging remains legal.

When owning it makes sense

The honest bit. Square’s free plan is hard to beat for one chair, and I’d tell you to start there. Your own booking makes sense when you want clients booking and paying deposits on your own website, under your name, with every client record in one place you own.
