Fair questions

What happens if I stop paying for care?

Your website stays yours. After the first three months you can stop any time: I hand over everything, help you move it to a host of your choice, and you pay that host directly from then on.

What does “one tool” mean?

One thing your website does for you, built in and working: online booking with deposits and reminders, quotes customers accept and pay a deposit on, online orders for collection or delivery, or instant replies on WhatsApp.

Who owns it?

You do. Your domain, words, photos and customer data are in your name from day one, and the code is yours once the build is paid.

Is the price fixed?

Yes. After our first conversation you get a fixed price in writing. It only changes if you ask for something new, and you’ll know the cost before I start on it.

Why is there a monthly fee?

Care pays for hosting, security, backups, fixes and one improvement every month, so the site keeps working and keeps getting better without surprise bills.

Is there a discount?

Founding clients get 30% off the build: 5 places, until 31 March 2027, in return for a short case study and permission to quote what they say. Their care price stays the same for their first 12 months. If you recommend a business and they go ahead, they get 10% off and you get a month of care free.
