Why it looks like this

Every colour on this site comes from frankincense or the place it grows in Oman. Hover over one.

  • Burner black#120D09

    The charcoal in an Omani incense burner. It's the background, so your work is the brightest thing on the screen.

  • Hojari pale#D7DCC7

    The colour of a top-grade tear, pale with a hint of green. It's the standard everything here is held to.

  • Honey#E3AD5C

    What resin looks like held up to the light. I use it for anything you can press.

  • Amber#B8661D

    Lower-grade resin, warmer and rougher. It turns up for contrast, the way Shaabi does in the grades.

  • Dhofar stone#E7E3DB

    The pale ground the trees grow out of. It's behind the pages you read, because it's easier on the eyes than bright white.

  • Khareef green#2B5A42

    Every summer the monsoon turns Dhofar green for a few weeks. It turns up here now and then, like a good surprise.

Hi, I'm Prejith.

Kerala is where my family's from, Muscat is where I grew up, and the UK is where I studied and worked. So when someone asks where I'm from, the honest answer takes a minute. I'm Indian, and Oman is home.

Why the name?

Hojari is the best frankincense in the world, and it only grows in Oman. I wanted a name that came from home, and a standard I'd have to live up to every time.

Why a marketer?

Most of my career has been in marketing. I've got an MSc in International Marketing from Coventry and a CMI Level 7, and I was Head of Marketing and Strategy at Performance Pathway. So every page I build has one job, getting someone to call you.

What I'm like to work with

Straight talking and quick to reply. If your website is already good, I'll say so. If it isn't, I'll tell you nicely and then fix it. I'll probably make you laugh at least once on the first call, too.

Fair questions

Do I pay anything upfront?

No. On Silver and Royal Green you pay monthly from the day your site goes live. Signature is a one-off build, paid half at the start and half at launch.

Why is it so affordable?

I'm starting out and building my first ten case studies, so the founding price is lower than it will be later. Lock it in now and it stays that way for as long as you're with me.

Is there a contract?

Nothing long. Stay three months, then cancel whenever you like with 30 days' notice. Pay yearly and you get two months free.

What if I don't like it?

You see your own homepage before you pay a penny. And if you're not happy in your first 30 days, I'll refund you.

Who owns the website?

You do. The domain is in your name and the content is yours, so if you ever leave, it all goes with you. Nobody's held hostage.

I already have a website. Can you improve it?

Yes. I'll look at it properly first, and if it's already good I'll tell you. Honesty is cheaper than a redesign you don't need.

Can you build apps too?

Yes. Booking systems, loyalty cards, customer portals and small tools that work on any phone. Proper App Store apps take longer and cost more, and I'll tell you honestly which one you need.

Who writes the words?

I do, from a 30-minute chat with you, and you approve every line. It'll sound like you on a good day, and nothing like a robot.

Where are you based?

Muscat, in Oman, which is home. Your website lives on the internet, so it doesn't mind. I lived in the UK for years and I keep UK hours for clients. And yes, I know what a Greggs sausage roll is.
