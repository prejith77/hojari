Fair questions

Everything people usually ask before they hire a web designer, answered plainly.

Cost and commitment

Do I pay anything upfront?

No. On Silver and Royal Green you pay monthly from the day your site goes live. Signature is a one-off build, paid in full or in three interest-free payments.

What's included, and are there any extra costs?

Design, words, hosting, security, backups, your domain and email for the first year, and unlimited small changes are all included. The only extra is your domain renewal after year one, usually £10 to £20 a year.

Is there a contract?

Nothing long. Stay three months, then cancel whenever you like with 30 days' notice. Pay yearly and you get two months free.

Will the price go up?

Not for founding clients. If you're one of my first ten businesses, your price stays the same for as long as you're with me.

Why is it so affordable?

I'm starting out and building my first ten case studies, so the founding price is lower than it will be later. You get the same work, at a better price, for being early.

What if I don't like it?

You see your own homepage before you pay a penny. And if you're not happy in your first 30 days after launch, I'll refund you.

How do I pay?

By card or bank transfer, monthly or yearly. You get a proper invoice for every payment.

The build

How long does it take?

Your homepage mock-up arrives within 48 hours. Once I have your words and photos, the whole site is live within five days.

What do you need from me?

A 15-minute chat, your logo if you have one, a few photos and your prices or services. I'll handle the rest, including writing the words.

Who writes the words?

I do, from our chat, and you approve every line. It'll sound like you on a good day, after a decent coffee.

I don't have good photos. Is that a problem?

No. I'll tell you exactly what to snap on your phone, and fill any gaps with good licensed photos. Signature includes help planning a proper shoot.

How many changes can I ask for before launch?

I keep going until you're happy with it. Most people need one or two rounds.

I already have a website. Can you improve it?

Yes. I'll look at it properly first, and if it's already good I'll tell you. I'll move your old content across for free.

I already own a domain. Can I keep it?

Yes. I connect it for you. You keep your current email address too.

Will it work with the booking system I already use?

Usually, yes. Fresha, Treatwell, Calendly, Square and most others can be linked or built right into your pages.

Can you build an online shop?

Yes, for small shops. A handful of products fits on Royal Green. A bigger shop is a Signature build, and I'll give you a fixed price before we start.

Can you build apps too?

Yes. Booking systems, loyalty cards, customer portals and small tools that work on any phone. App Store apps take longer and cost more, and most businesses don't need one.

After launch

Will I show up on Google?

I set up what Google looks for, including your Google Business Profile, fast pages, the right titles and descriptions, and on Royal Green, pages for the areas you cover. Nobody can honestly promise you first place, and you should run from anyone who does.

Can I make changes myself?

You can, but most people just WhatsApp me. Small changes are unlimited and done within 48 hours. If you'd like to update things like opening hours yourself, I can set that up.

Is it secure, and is it backed up?

Yes. Every site has a secure padlock connection, is kept up to date and is backed up. If anything ever breaks, I fix it.

What about privacy rules and cookies?

I write your privacy page for you, and I don't add tracking cookies unless you need them, so most sites don't need an annoying cookie banner.

Is it accessible?

Yes. Text is readable, colours have strong contrast, and the site works with screen readers and keyboards.

Will I know if it is working?

Royal Green includes a short monthly report in plain English, covering visits, calls, bookings and what I'd change next.

Do you do social media or Google Ads?

Not as a service. I'll make sure your site is ready for them, and if you ask, I'll tell you whether they're worth your money.

About working with me

Who owns the website?

Your domain is in your name, and your words and photos are always yours. After 12 months the design is yours too. Leave before that and you can buy it outright, or take your words and photos with you. Signature sites are yours from day one.

Where are you based?

Muscat, in Oman, which is home. Your website lives on the internet, so it doesn't mind. I lived in the UK for years and keep UK hours for clients. And yes, I know what a Greggs sausage roll is.

Do we need to meet in person?

No. Everything happens over a video call, WhatsApp and email.

What happens if you are ill or on holiday?

Your site keeps running on its own, and everything is backed up. I'll always tell you in advance if I'm away, and I still do urgent fixes from wherever I am.

Do you only work with UK businesses?

No. I build for UK businesses first, but I live in Muscat, so the Gulf is home turf too. Prices here are in pounds; if you're in Oman, the UAE or nearby, I'll quote in rials or dirhams. I can also build your site in English and Arabic, with the Arabic reading right to left as it should, rather than just flipped.

Why should I trust someone I've just found?

You don't need to trust me yet. You see your homepage before paying, there's nothing upfront and you can have your money back in the first 30 days. If anyone's taking a gamble here, it's me.
