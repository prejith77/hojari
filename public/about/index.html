Where I’m from

I’m a Malayali, raised between Kerala, Muscat and England. England meant Papworth Everard, then Coventry for my master’s, then Birmingham. Today I live in Muscat. I live in Muscat (GMT+4), so my working day overlaps with the Gulf, India, Europe and the UK, and I reply to everyone, wherever they are, within one working day.

In my day job I run the ordering, stock and suppliers for a business with more than 9,000 products. The tools I built there gave my team back hours every week. Then I kept seeing the same thing in other businesses: owners answering messages at midnight, and staff typing the same details into three apps, when a simple system could do that work for them. That’s the work Hojari does.

How it works

  1. Tell me about your business
