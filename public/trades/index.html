Fair questions

How much does a website for a tradesperson cost?

With Hojari, from £750 for a website that sends quotes and takes deposits for you, then £49 a month for care. If you already have a website, the one-week fix adds quoting to it from £450.

What about jobs I can’t price online?

Customers book a visit instead. You see the job, then send a fixed price before any work starts.

Can customers pay by card?

Yes, through a payment provider such as Stripe, straight into your own account. Your provider’s card fee applies; in the UK, Stripe charges 1.5% + 20p on standard cards.

What does a client area look like?

Like the client portal demo: dates, drawings, payments and messages for one job, in one place.

Do I own it?

Yes. Your domain, your words, your photos and your customer data are in your name from day one, and the code is yours once the build is paid.
